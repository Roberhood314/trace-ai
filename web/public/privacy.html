TRACE-AI is designed to process only information necessary for authorized search, missing-person, rescue, or case-management workflows. Access to case data is role-controlled and logged.

Data

The application may process Pi account identifiers, case records, authorized location events, descriptive information, and evidence files uploaded by authorized users. It does not require a Pi passphrase and should never ask for one.

Purpose and access

Data is used to manage cases, construct timelines, visualize authorized search zones, and provide decision-support summaries. Sensitive data must only be supplied where the operator has a lawful basis and appropriate authorization.

Retention and deletion

The deploying organization must configure retention, deletion, backup, and incident-response rules appropriate to its legal obligations. Development/demo data should not contain real personal data.
